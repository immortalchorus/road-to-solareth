    Tank Driver Arrow keys drive; diagonal steering banks the tank automatically. Hold F to climb; release F to hold altitude. Hold Shift + F to freefall; release either key to hover at the current altitude. P toggles autopilot; Shift + P changes its altitude phase. Space drops bombs; 1 toggles bombing sonar; 2 toggles nose-down attack attitude; M launches rack missiles. F1/F2/F3 select missile range; G toggles the tactical grid. Shift + Left/Right yaws turret. Y + Up/Down elevates cannon. V centers the turret; Shift + V preserves pitch. Ctrl fires; hold Z for seeking ammo. Heavy fire calls Wingman 1 and Wingman 2 into formation. Press 5 to order them home and 7 to authorize fuel and missile resupply. Tab toggles camera height. Escape clears nearby obstacles.
